The scores come from different accounts or from reports without an identified account. They cannot be combined into one official total. [Results and evidence](../docs/results.md) explains the source of each score and the remaining uncertainty. The [submission index](../runs/submissions.csv) lists each claim; its rows are not all website-verified attempts.

The problem guides organize the work for readers. Original CSVs, explanations, receipts, candidate manifests, and account folders remain at their recorded paths. There are **11 distinct retained vectors**. [retention.json](retention.json) maps eight removed duplicate files to their retained equivalents and Git recovery records. Historical receipt paths remain unchanged, and numerical equivalence is never treated as proof of identical uploaded bytes.
