Empirical-geometry prior, not a simulation of the hidden circuit. Row ordering comes from the circuit itself: 35 native mps.gpu chi128 jobs, each returning all 38 <Z_j> (job IDs in the manifest), give a contact map m_j = mean_q <Z_j>; rows are ranked by m_j (late-contact rows keep <Z_j> closest to +1 in the truncated echo) and assigned the light-cone slack distribution of the published 6x6+(36,37), 16-layer, butterfly-q0 geometry by rank. Each row's value is the pooled exact OTOC(2) class mean for that slack from exact statevector simulations of smaller seeded instances of the same circuit generator (4x4, 4x5, 5x5; depths 8/12/16), isotonic in slack. The overall sign is set positive to match the complete native level-3 Pauli-path (delta 1e-4) vector on the late-contact rows. No extrapolation; magnitudes are ensemble means (|v| <= 0.1) and only the vector shape is meaningful.
