Ran each of the 38 published circuits (Duke_G4_0–37) separately on BlueQubit's Pauli-path simulator, measuring the target qubit's ⟨Z⟩. Every circuit used the same settings: truncation threshold ε=0.0001, transpilation level 3. We tightened ε step by step (0.01 → 0.001 → 0.0003 → 0.0001) on a few test qubits. At coarser ε most values collapsed to exactly 0; at 0.0001 only 4 of 38 do. No extrapolation, combining of methods, or tuning based on scores. These are truncated-simulation estimates without guaranteed error bounds.
